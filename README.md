# Unified 2 – Governed AI Decision Layer

Unified 2 is a governance and audit layer for AI-assisted decision processes.

It ensures that every AI recommendation is:
- evaluated against explicit policies
- reviewed by a human when required
- documented with a complete, exportable audit trail

**Unified 2 does not replace human decision-making.**  
It proves that decisions were made in a controlled, accountable way.

---

## Why Unified 2 exists

As AI systems are increasingly used in regulated environments, organizations face a growing challenge:

> How do you prove that an AI-assisted decision was compliant, explainable, and human-approved?

Unified 2 addresses this problem by placing governance at the center of AI execution.

---

## What Unified 2 provides

- **Deterministic decision states** – Every decision follows a defined lifecycle
- **Explicit policy enforcement** – Rules are code, not documentation
- **Human-in-the-loop escalation** – High-risk actions require human approval
- **Immutable audit events** – Complete, tamper-evident audit trail
- **Exportable audit bundles** – Evidence packages for regulatory review

---

## What Unified 2 does NOT do

- ❌ No autonomous decision-making
- ❌ No black-box reasoning
- ❌ No replacement of human responsibility

**All final decisions remain with a human actor.**

## Architecture

Unified 2 follows a **governance-first architecture**:

```
Input → AI Recommendation → Policy Evaluation → Decision State → (Optional) Human Review → Final Status → Audit Export
```

See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) for details.

## Security

Unified 2 is designed for use in security- and compliance-sensitive environments.

Key principles:
- Explicit policy enforcement
- Deterministic state machine
- Mandatory audit logging
- Separation of inference and decision layers
