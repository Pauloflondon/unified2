## Decision Responsibility

**All decision responsibility remains with the user.**

Unified 2 provides governance infrastructure and audit trails, but:

- **Policy Definition** – Customer defines policies (allow/deny rules)
- **Risk Thresholds** – Customer sets risk thresholds (when to trigger HITL)
- **HITL Decisions** – Customer's admins approve/reject actions
- **Audit Ownership** – Customer owns audit logs and evidence exports

**NovaPact provides the governance infrastructure; the customer retains full decision authority.**
